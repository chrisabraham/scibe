20:15) Judy Malloy 28-AUG-94

Also named in this lexia: Anna Couey.

I push the elastic yellow scrambled eggs around on my tray with my fork
so that it appears that I have eaten some
while on Arts Wire,
Anna Couey dreams of murderers,
looks out the WELL window at the peaceful Sausalito harbor,
tells a story about exchanging net access for coffee
at Woodstock.
