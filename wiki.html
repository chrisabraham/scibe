The Scibe wiki

My Name is Scibe was written in 1994, in two rooms--one on The WELL, one on ARTS WIRE--at the moment hypertext became the web. This wiki is the preservation edition’s context layer: fifteen sourced entries on the work, the form it belongs to, the field that studies it, the medium of online conferencing it was written in, the software under its rooms, the networks and the company that carried it, the village of hosts and makers who ran them, and the eight people who wrote it. Each entry stands on the public record and ends with the sources consulted for it; what the record does not support is not written here. The sister work written in TMN’s rooms, Item 8, keeps its own context at item8.org.
