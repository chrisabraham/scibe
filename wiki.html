The Scibe wiki

My Name is Scibe was written in 1994, in two rooms—one on The WELL, one on ARTS WIRE—at the moment hypertext became the web. This wiki is the preservation edition’s reference annex: a set of sourced entries on the work, the form it belongs to, the field that studies it, the networks and the company that carried it, and the eight people who wrote it. Each entry stands on the public record and ends with the sources consulted for it; what the record does not support is not written here.
