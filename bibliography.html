scibe> bibliography — every source, with its citing pages

This page gathers every source the edition stands on, in one place, like the end notes of a book. Each entry names what its source establishes for this edition and how it was reached — read in full, read, attested in another record, given as testimony, or surviving as no public document at all — and each entry ends by naming the pages that cite it, so a reader can walk from any claim in the edition back to its paper.

The edition's aim, in its editor's words, is this: “To stitch this all together as best as possible, to be an historical resource, with the hypertext writing on top as cream on top.” The bibliography is that aim's apparatus: the stitching made visible, source by source.

Item 8's bibliography gathers the same village's other half of the paper: item8.org/bibliography.html.
