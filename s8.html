20:6) Judy Malloy 19-AUG-94

The white cardbox also contains a green kazoo, bubble soap and two bubble blowing pipes. These things were sent to me by Larry. "When I was a kid, I was pretty much labelled with a permanent outpatient status. When I would come home from the Emergency room with a new cast or a new set of stitches, Mom would always buy me a toy.." he wrote.

Under the white cardboard box there is brown folder containing the witness statements of the accident. Maybe today is the day that I will read them.
