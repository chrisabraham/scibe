20:38) Judy Malloy 06-OCT-94

Also named in this lexia: Joseph Wilson.

In the phone booth, I watched the black case
that contained my laptop carefully,
leaning my sliver metal crutches against the black metal telephone
while I fumbled for change.
"Yesterday I realized that Popes have laps
just like everyone else,"
Joseph Wilson said on topic 20.

There was no jack in that telephone booth
that Valerie couldn't find
so I had to speak into the receiver
although I would rather have used my fingers
on the keyboard.
"We live in a home page," Chris' friend had said.
