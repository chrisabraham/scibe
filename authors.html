scibe> who — the eight writers

The eight writers, in the order they first appear in the work. Judy Malloy comes first because the work is hers—she originated and orchestrated it; the other seven are her contributors, credited here in full and in order. Under each name are the lexias whose posting header carries that name; where a writer is named inside another writer’s lexia, that is noted too. Nothing here goes beyond what the work itself records. Each lexia page carries its own reading, in the voice cast for its writer.

The main artist. She originated this work, set it in motion from her sickbed in Tempe, and orchestrated the collaboration that gathered around her—Scibe is her narrator and her voice, and the lexias that carry no other writer’s header—the first, the last, and the strand that runs between them—are hers. The seven writers below are contributors to her work.
