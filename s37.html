20:32) Valerie Gardiner 30-SEP-94

I made a reservation for a room with two double beds. Alice got the key from the desk. We took the elevator. Our room was large, but crammed with ten double beds. Alice and I were assigned one of the beds.

A middle-aged blond and a barely-grown man sat close together on the last bed in the row. I walked up and spoke to them. I wanted to be friendly, to make the best of an uncomfortable situation.

They turned and glared at me.
